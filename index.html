QUESTIONS

Before we hit render.

How is this different from a regular video shoot?

No crew, no location, no shoot day. Every frame is generated using AI production tools, which means a concept that would take a traditional agency four to six weeks and five figures takes us a day at a fraction of the cost. The work is still directed, scripted, graded and cut by a human — the generation is just the camera.

What do you actually need from me to start?

Less than you'd think. A product photo or two, your logo, and a sentence about who you're selling to. If you have brand guidelines, references or a specific hook in mind, send those too. If you don't, we'll propose a concept and you approve it before we build anything.

How fast can you deliver?

A single ad takes 24 hours. A three-video pack takes three days. A full campaign runs five to seven. Rush delivery is available on most projects — ask when you enquire. Speed is the point of working this way.

What formats do I get?

You get the format you asked for — 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1 — not all three by default. Captions burned in plus a clean version without. Those are the sizes Meta wants for Reels, Feed and Stories. Need a second crop later? Ask when you enquire.

Do I own the videos? Can I run them as paid ads?

Yes. Full commercial usage rights on delivery — paid ads, organic posts, website, email, anywhere. No licensing windows, no per-platform fees, no coming back to us for permission when the campaign scales.

What if I don't like the first version?

Revision rounds are included in every package. Send your notes in one list and we'll turn them around fast. If the direction is fundamentally wrong we'd rather rebuild the concept than polish something you don't believe in — that conversation is free.
